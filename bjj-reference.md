# BJJ reference

Visual cues for the bjj-silver position vocabulary. Positions are neutral: they do not say who is on top or who the user is.

- open guard: bottom person on their back or seat, legs between the two, feet or knees on the partner, no closed lock.
- closed guard: bottom person's ankles crossed around the partner's waist or back.
- half guard: bottom person traps one of the partner's legs between their own legs.
- butterfly guard: bottom person seated, both feet hooked inside the partner's thighs, often with underhooks.
- side control: top person chest-down across the partner's torso from the side, perpendicular.
- mount: top person sits astride the partner's torso, knees on both sides.
- back control: one person behind the other with hooks or a body triangle, chest to their back.
- turtle: one person on hands and knees, curled up, partner beside or behind.
- standing: both on their feet, grips or no grips.
- scramble: fast transition, no stable top or bottom, limbs moving.
- leg entanglement: legs wrapped together (e.g. ankle or knee lock entries, 50/50, saddle), often both seated or lying.
- not visible: the pair is out of frame or hidden. Skip it.
